My work sits in theoretical computer science, with a focus on **algebraic complexity theory** and **computational complexity**. I study algebraic models of computation, the complexity of polynomials, and the structure of algebraic and combinatorial problems.

    <h2>Algebraic complexity theory (ACT)</h2>
    <p class="topic-description">
      I study the power and limits of algebraic models of computation: arithmetic
      circuits, polynomial identity testing, division and truncation, polynomial
      interpolation, and the geometric complexity theory program. A recurring theme
      is what algebraic algorithms can do without randomness, and how algebraic
      natural proofs and border complexity constrain lower-bound techniques.

    <p class="eyebrow">02</p>
    <h2>Computational complexity (CC)</h2>
    <p class="topic-description">
      I study the structural and algorithmic complexity of algebraic and
      combinatorial problems: NP-hardness, parameterized complexity, real
      feasibility, PosSLP, sums of squares, and the complexity of deciding
      polynomial systems. Recent work connects Hilbert's Nullstellensatz and
      multivariate resultants to optimization, counting hierarchies, and real
      feasibility.
    </p>
