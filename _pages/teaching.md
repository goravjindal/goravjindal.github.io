      <h2>Algebraic Complexity Theory</h2>
      <p class="course-meta">University of Regensburg · Co-instructor with {% include person-link.html key="bhargav" name="Bhargav CS" %} and {% include person-link.html key="shukla" name="Himanshu Shukla" %}</p>
      <details class="course-details">
        <summary>Details</summary>
        <div class="details-body">
          <p>A graduate introduction to algebraic and geometric complexity theory, following the first twelve chapters of Bläser and Ikenmeyer’s <a href="https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/gs010/gs010.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><em>Introduction to Geometric Complexity Theory</em></a>. The goal is to develop the algebraic, geometric, and representation-theoretic tools needed to study the permanent-versus-determinant problem and the border rank of matrix multiplication.</p>
          <p><strong>My role:</strong> Taught Boolean and arithmetic circuits, Waring rank, orbit closures, and introductory algebraic geometry. Designed and graded exercise sheets; conducted and graded the oral exam.</p>
        </div>
      </details>
